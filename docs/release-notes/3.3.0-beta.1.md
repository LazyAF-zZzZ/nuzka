> **เวอร์ชันทดสอบ / Test version**

- overlay ใหม่หกอัน (เพิ่มใน OBS sources): Starting soon, Be right back, Ending, VS screen, Lower third และ Scoreboard พร้อมพื้นหลังเคลื่อนไหว หกสไตล์ สิบสองชุดสี

- Six new overlays (in OBS sources): Starting soon, Be right back, Ending, VS screen, Lower third and
  Scoreboard, with an animated background (six styles, twelve colour sets).
- A new "Scenes and lower third" card at the bottom of the Control Panel: lower third, countdown,
  background and scene wording.
- Oxanium and Rajdhani fonts are included and can be picked in the overlay editor.
- The new pages use the same layout and colour editors as the other overlays.
