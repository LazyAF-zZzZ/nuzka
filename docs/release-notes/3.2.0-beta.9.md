## New name: Nuzka

With a new icon.

ROV Overlay Tool is now called **Nuzka**. Your teams, tournaments, drafts and settings are all
still here, updates keep arriving the same way, and your OBS scenes work unchanged.

## Supporters and the watermark

Every feature stays free.

- Every overlay shows a watermark: a small app logo followed by **Nuzka Â· by LazyAF**.
  On the draft overlay it sits inside the banner, next to the red ban slots; elsewhere it is in a corner.
- A new **Support** screen (above Settings): press **Get a key Â· à¸¿159**, pay by PromptPay or card through Stripe, and the key appears at once (this test build uses Stripe test mode: no real money).
- **Supporters** get a key starting with `RVS1-`. Paste it on the **Support** screen or in **Settings** and the
  watermark disappears from every overlay at once.
- The key is checked on this PC: no account, and no internet needed during a broadcast.
- A reminder appears 7 days before a key runs out. After that the watermark comes back;
  nothing else changes.
- Settings now says the app is not endorsed by Garena or Tencent.

> If the watermark covers anything in OBS, say so and it will be moved.
